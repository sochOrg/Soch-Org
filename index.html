About
Us

Founded on July 24, 2018, by Shiwath Rai, SOCH (formerly DSS Colony) evolved from a regional fashion community into a powerful platform for society building. We empower the youth of the hills to shape their own future through art, business, and impactful media.

Evolution

From a fashion page to a multi-faceted regional engine for content and community growth.

Core Purpose

Catering professional art and business platforms specifically to the regional hills community.

Our Nature
